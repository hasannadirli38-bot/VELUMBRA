Privacy Policy

Version 3 · Effective 17 November 2026

This policy explains which personal data the Velumbra mobile app (“the app”) processes, why and how, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and how you can exercise your rights. It is written to match what the app actually does today. If the Turkish and English versions differ, the Turkish version prevails.

1. Controller

NADIRLI (individual developer). Contact: velumbra.review@gmail.com

2. In short

  • To interpret your dream and create a frame from it, your voice recording and dream text are sent to our AI provider OpenAI. This processing takes place outside your country, mainly in the United States.
  • Your dreams are stored on Supabase infrastructure on servers in Frankfurt, Germany, linked only to your account. Other users cannot see them.
  • Once transcribed, your voice recording is deleted automatically 7 days after recording by a cleanup job that runs daily.
  • No ads, no sale of data, no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • You can delete a dream or your whole account from inside the app at any time.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the service (storing, transcribing, interpreting, generating images, conversations, letters, rewrites, the book): performance of a contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c; GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Dream content may include special categories of data such as health or sex life. We ask for your explicit consent on first launch to process this content with our AI provider and transfer it abroad (KVKK Arts. 6 and 9; GDPR Arts. 9(2)(a) and 49(1)(a)). You can withdraw consent by deleting your account or writing to us; interpretation will then no longer be available.
  • Subscriptions: performance of a contract.
  • Preventing abuse and security (daily limits, technical logs): legitimate interests (KVKK Art. 5/2-f; GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Legal obligations (KVKK Art. 5/2-ç; GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)).

The AI reading does not make decisions about you that produce legal or similarly significant effects; it is only a text shown to you.

What goes to OpenAI and how long it stays

  • Sent: your recording (for transcription); your dream text, titles and symbols of your recent dreams, the symbol names that recur most in your earlier dreams, your night intention, sun sign, waking feeling, “You in the frames” choice and app language if present (for the reading); an image description and, if you chose one, your “You in the frames” value (for the image); your figure messages; the week’s dream notes (for the letter); your dream text and chosen moment (for rewrites). Instead of your identity, an irreversible hash derived from your user ID is sent (for abuse tracking); your email is never sent.
  • Training: according to OpenAI’s API documentation, since 1 March 2023 data sent to the API is not used to train OpenAI models (unless the customer explicitly opts in to share it; we do not).
  • Retention: according to OpenAI’s documentation, content of transcription requests is not retained. For reading, conversation, letter, rewrite and image requests OpenAI keeps abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days (longer if required by law or to prevent harm). We send reading, conversation, letter and rewrite requests so that the response is not stored by OpenAI as application data (store: false).

6. International transfers

OpenAI and RevenueCat are in the United States; Supabase’s servers are in the EU but the company is in the US. Your data is therefore transferred outside Türkiye and the European Economic Area. Transfers are based on the providers’ data processing agreements and standard contractual clauses, and on the explicit consent you give on first launch.

7. Retention

  • Dreams and related content (text, reading, image, feeling, intention, conversations, rewrites, letters): until you delete them or your account.
  • Voice recordings: if transcription succeeded, deleted 7 days after recording by a cleanup job that runs daily. Recordings of dreams that could not be transcribed or whose processing failed are kept, so that you can retry, until you delete the dream or your account.
  • When you delete a dream, its recording, image, reading, conversations and rewrite are deleted immediately. Usage records without content remain until your account is deleted (so allowances cannot be reset).
  • When you delete your account, all your data and files are deleted from our servers immediately. At the same time we ask RevenueCat to permanently delete your subscription record (RevenueCat completes the deletion shortly; if this step fails because of an error, just write to us). They may remain in automatic backups for a short time (one backup cycle).
  • OpenAI: as described in section 5 (no retention for transcription; up to 30 days otherwise).
  • Technical logs: for the provider’s log period, a few days.
  • Use without a linked account (anonymous): if you uninstall the app, your session is lost and that data can no longer be reached; it remains on the server. Before uninstalling, we recommend deleting your data via Profile → Delete my account.

8. Deletion

  • In the app: for a dream, the card’s “⋯” menu → Delete; for everything, Profile → Delete my account. Deletion is immediate.
  • On the web: Account deletion page.
  • By email: velumbra.review@gmail.com. If your account is linked to Google, writing from that address lets us verify you. For anonymous accounts we cannot verify identity, so please delete from inside the app.

Your subscription lives in Google Play; deleting your account does not cancel it. To cancel: Google Play → Subscriptions.

9. Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 (Türkiye): to learn whether your data is processed and request information about it, to learn the purpose and whether data is used accordingly, to know the third parties it is transferred to, to request correction, deletion or destruction and notification of these to recipients, to object to a result against you arising from automated analysis, and to claim compensation for damage from unlawful processing.

Under the GDPR (if you are in the EU/EEA): rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection and withdrawal of consent.

Write to velumbra.review@gmail.com; we respond within 30 days at the latest. You can complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or, in the EU, to the data protection authority of your country.

10. Ads and sale of data

The app has no ads. We do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers (SDKs). Fonts are bundled in the app; it makes no font requests to external servers.

11. Security

Connections are encrypted (TLS). The database uses row-level security: each user can access only their own data. Only the server can write readings and subscription status. Secret keys live on the server, not in the app. You can lock the app with your phone’s authentication. No system is perfect; if a breach occurs we will notify as required by law.

12. Age limit

Velumbra is for people aged 18 and over. On first launch you declare that you are over 18. If we learn that we process data of someone under 18, we delete it.
