Delete your account and data

Velumbra · developer: NADIRLI

In the app (recommended, immediate)

  1. Open Velumbra.
  2. Tap the profile icon at the top right.
  3. Tap Delete my account at the bottom and confirm in two steps.

Deletion is immediate. To delete a single dream only: open the dream’s card → “⋯” at the top right → Delete.

By email

If you cannot access the app, email hasan.nadirli38@gmail.com with the subject “Velumbra account deletion”.

  • If your account is linked to Google, write from that Google email address. We will delete your account and all your data within 30 days at the latest and reply to you.
  • If your account is not linked to Google (anonymous), it is recognised only by the session on your phone and carries no email address, so we cannot verify your identity and find the right account. Please delete from inside the app.

What is deleted

  • Your account and email address (if any)
  • Your voice recordings, dream texts, readings and dream images
  • Your waking feelings, night intentions, figure conversations, rewrites and weekly letters
  • Your sun sign, consent record and usage records
  • Your subscription record at RevenueCat (your random user ID and purchase history): a permanent deletion request is sent at the same time, and RevenueCat completes it shortly.

What may remain

  • Data may remain in automatic backups for a short time (one backup cycle) and is then removed automatically.
  • Your purchase records at Google Play are subject to Google’s own policy.
  • Content previously sent to OpenAI may remain in OpenAI’s abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days (longer if required by law).

Your subscription lives in Google Play; deleting your account does not cancel it. To cancel: Google Play → Subscriptions.
